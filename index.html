Systems for the 505

Design and installation of custom control systems — rake, ram, pole launchers, jib and vang setups — tuned to how you sail and led exactly where you want them.

Splicing

Precision rope work in Dyneema and modern cordage: halyards, continuous control lines, soft shackles, strops, and tapers. Light, strong, and clean.

Calibration

Rig measurement and marked calibration for rake, tension, and controls, so your settings are repeatable and your tuning guide actually means something on the water.

Carbon Work

Carbon fibre fabrication and repair — tiller extensions, fittings, reinforcement, and structural repairs done properly and finished cleanly.

Built Around One Boat: The 505

The 505 is one of the most rewarding — and most complicated — dinghies to rig well. Every system interacts with the next, and small details make big differences on the race course.

I focus exclusively on the 505 class, which means every job benefits from class-specific knowledge: what works, what breaks, and what the fast boats in the fleet are doing right now.
